## S4PRED

The container can install [S4PRED](https://github.com/psipred/s4pred) for
single-sequence secondary-structure prediction. S4PRED is GPL-3.0 software and
must be distributed under its own license terms. Cite Moffat and Jones (2021),
*Bioinformatics* 37(21):3744-3751, https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btab337.

are responsible for the terms, privacy policies, and citation requirements of
those services.
